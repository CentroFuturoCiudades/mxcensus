## The definitions found

- **Household size** = `TOTPERS`; **type** = `TIPOHOG` (1–4 familiar: nuclear, ampliado,
  compuesto, not specified; 5–6 no familiar: unipersonal, corresidente; 9 not specified).
- **Household income** (`INGTRHOG`, `INGTOHOG`): 999999 = not specified, 999998 the top code
  (above 10 wages); the groups as the persons' (C2KEM08) but **exactly 3 wages fall in «más
  de 2 hasta menos de 3»** — Coahuila's total incomes settle it (15.253 against 15.25, the
  strict bound 15.218); the other bounds as published.
- **HO08**: the head = `OTROPARE_C` 100, his or her age of the heads of a specified age; the
  active = the 12+ working or looking for work (`CONACT_CAT`), the inactive «No trabaja».
- **ED12**: never attended = `ABANESCO` 0, stopped attending = 1–8 (its causes), not
  specified = 9, of the 7–29 not attending (`ASISTEN` 2).
