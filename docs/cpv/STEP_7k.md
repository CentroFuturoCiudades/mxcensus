## The definitions found

- **Disability** (DI01/02/04): without = `DISNOTIE` 8, not specified = `DISMOV` 9, with = any
  type. The types (shares of the disabled; a person may have several) are the items' own
  codes and the classified «other» limitation `DISOTRA_C`: **motor** = `DISMOV` 1, `DISBRA` 2
  or `DISOTRA_C` 299 (insufficiently specified motor), **mental** = `DISMENT` 6 or 399,
  **the type not specified** = 960 (even beside another type), **other** = 131
  (communication and language — not «del lenguaje», which is `DISMUDO` 4 only), 199, 421,
  422, 430, 499, and the second limitation that could not be classified (`DISOTRA_V` 7, «tiene
  dos limitaciones y una no se pudo clasificar»: it closes the «other» gap of Baja
  California, Durango, Nayarit and Tamaulipas — 0.11 → 0.00). The other readings tried (131 as
  speech, 960 only without another type, 199/499 as not specified) miss by up to 0.1–0.2
  points outside Chiapas.
- **Fertility**: the women of 12 and more who said how many children they had born alive
  (`NUMHIJ` below 98); the surviving children (`HIJSOB`) count 0 where the woman gave none
  (the item is asked of mothers); the children who died (`HIJFAL`) as % of those born alive,
  of the women who gave both.
- **Language** (LI02): the 5+ speaking one (`HLENGUA` 1), then Spanish (`HESPANOL` 3, 4, 9)
  as % of them; not speaking one (2), not specified (9).
